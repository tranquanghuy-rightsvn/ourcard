Our Story

Not sure how to spend your time in Kyu Craft | Popup Card? We have provided and manufactured 3D pop-up cards for our customers with the hope to spread Vietnamese people’s beauty, ingenuity, warmth, and generosity to recipients all over the world. Would you like to join our journey?

Kyu Craft | Popup Card’s story. In the context of complete turmoil and full of physical and mental difficulties after covid, our Kyu Craft | Popup Card team was born with the desire of bringing spiritual food through 3D hand-made cards to every corner in the world, because more than ever we understand the value of sharing the great love sent through each card when we can't see each other.

Working and dedicating with the motto as well as the core value "Faster – Better – Cheaper", we not only would like to reach the position of the top company in the industry, but more than ever, we strive to bring quality products and the most perfect service to touch every customer's heart.

Kyu Craft | Popup Card might not be anything without you, dear customers, but we will be really strong, thrive and overcome all challenges when we have your companion, support and trust.

Kyu Craft | Popup Card’s product. Our range of premium quality pop up cards are all lovingly designed in Vietnam and then hand assembled, creating a keepsake that will leave a lasting impression on the lucky recipient.
