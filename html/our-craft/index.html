THE PROCESS

Draw mockup

We will discuss with the customers for the ideas and requests how to make new design. After that, the 2D designer will first sketch it.

Design

After customers confirm the sketch, we will go on making 2D and 3D

Laser cut

When the design file is workable, we will make a physical sample test for customer’s approval.

Handmade assembly

Finally, we will go on production after having customer’s approval. In case of not, we will discuss together to modify the design more.
