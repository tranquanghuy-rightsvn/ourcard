A whole stretch of coastline folded into a 5x7 card. Open it and the beach huts, palm leaves and rolling waves rise into a layered scene that stands on its own — close it again and the whole thing lies flat enough to post in a standard envelope.

Every layer is laser-cut, then scored, folded and glued by hand in our workshop, so the pop-up opens cleanly at 90 degrees and holds its shape. Blank inside for your own message.

Product information

  • Product code: PC1052
  • Size: 5 x 7 inch (closed)
  • Material: 250gsm art paper, laser-cut layers
  • Packaging: one envelope and one protective sleeve per card
  • Carton details: 50 pcs per box, 500 pcs per carton

Production takes 7–10 working days after the artwork and deposit are confirmed. We ship worldwide by air or sea from Ho Chi Minh, and can deliver to your forwarder if you have one. Sample orders go out by express courier.
Every batch is checked by hand before it leaves the workshop. If any card arrives damaged or does not match the approved sample, send us photos within 7 days of delivery and we will replace it or credit the amount against your next order.
