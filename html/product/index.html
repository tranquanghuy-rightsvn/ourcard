Information

Open this card and a whole stretch of coastline rises off the page — beach huts, palm leaves and rolling waves layered into a scene that stands on its own. Close it again and everything lies flat enough to post in a standard envelope.

Every layer is laser-cut, then scored, folded and glued by hand in our workshop in Ha Noi, so the pop-up opens cleanly at ninety degrees and holds its shape. The inside is left blank for your own message, and each card ships with an envelope and a protective sleeve.

KHT Card manufactures handmade 3D pop-up cards for retailers, distributors and gift shops worldwide. We also take on custom pop-up design and private-label production for wholesale customers — send us your artwork and we will build it into a working pop-up.
