Wholesale Orders

Looking to bring Kyu Craft pop-up cards to your store or distribution network? We’d love to work with you.

To request a wholesale quote, simply send us your product selection, estimated quantities, and shipping destination at:

Email: admin@kyucraft.com

Our standard wholesale order requirements are:

  • Minimum quantity: 50 pieces per design
  • Minimum total order: 500 pieces
  • Different designs can be mixed within the same order

Once we receive your request, the Kyu Craft team will review production availability and provide you with a quotation, estimated shipping cost, and payment details for your approval.

Each quotation will remain valid for the period stated on the quote. Pricing may be adjusted if there are changes to quantities, designs, packaging requirements, or the shipping destination.

Product & Brand Customization

Want to make our cards a better fit for your brand? Kyu Craft offers a range of customization options for wholesale orders.

Available options may include:

  • Printed or laser-cut logos
  • Barcodes, SKUs, and product labels
  • Custom packaging stickers and labels
  • Website, company information, or brand messaging
  • Color adjustments to existing designs
  • Customized printing on cards or envelopes

Your branding can be added to the card, envelope, or packaging, depending on the design and production requirements.

Some customization options may require a separate minimum order quantity or additional cost. We’ll always confirm these details with you before you place your order.

Custom Design & OEM Services

Looking for something completely unique?

Kyu Craft offers custom pop-up card design and OEM production for brands, retailers, museums, events, corporate projects, and special gift collections.

Getting started is easy. Simply share any of the following with us:

  • Your idea or concept
  • Logo and brand guidelines
  • Artwork or illustrations
  • Reference images
  • Preferred size or special requirements

Our design team will turn your idea into a custom pop-up card while recommending solutions that work well for both design and production.

Minimum order quantity for custom designs: 300 pieces per design.

Design, sample development, or prototype fees may apply depending on the complexity of the project.

For custom and OEM projects, we will provide a design proof or sample for your approval before moving forward with mass production.

Please get in touch with us to discuss pricing, sample development, and estimated project timelines.

Standard Packaging

Each standard Kyu Craft card includes:

  • 1 pop-up card
  • 1 matching envelope
  • 1 individual clear protective bag

Every product is carefully inspected and securely packed before leaving our facility to help keep your order protected during transit.

Need something different? We can also discuss custom packaging, branded labels, gift boxes, or special packing requirements when preparing your quotation.

Production Time

Our standard production time is approximately 10–35 business days after your order, design details, and deposit have been confirmed.

Production time may vary depending on:

  • Order quantity
  • Design complexity
  • Level of customization
  • Packaging requirements
  • Seasonal production demand

We will provide an estimated production schedule before your order is confirmed.

Please note that production time does not include international shipping or customs clearance time in the destination country.

Design Approval & Production Confirmation

For customized or OEM products, you will have the opportunity to review and approve the final design before mass production begins.

Production will be based on the final version approved by you.

Before approval, we recommend carefully checking:

  • Text and spelling
  • Logo and brand details
  • Colors
  • Dimensions
  • Barcodes
  • Printed content
  • Packaging specifications

Once the design has been approved and production has started, some changes may no longer be possible or may involve additional costs and production time.

Payment Terms

Our standard payment terms are:

  • 50% deposit to confirm the order and begin production
  • 50% balance before shipment

Kyu Craft currently accepts payment in USD by bank transfer, PayPal, and other international payment methods agreed upon by both parties.

Production will begin once we have received the deposit and all required order details have been confirmed.

Any bank charges or international transaction fees, where applicable, will depend on the selected payment method and will be communicated when necessary.

International Shipping

Kyu Craft ships wholesale orders to customers around the world.

Depending on your order size, destination, and preferred delivery time, available shipping options may include:

  • International express courier services such as DHL, UPS, or FedEx
  • Air freight
  • Sea freight

Shipping costs are calculated separately based on the carton dimensions, total weight, destination, and shipping method.

If you’re unsure which option is best, our team will be happy to help you compare shipping methods and find a suitable balance between delivery time and cost.

Import Duties & Taxes

Depending on your destination country or region, your shipment may be subject to import duties, VAT/GST, customs fees, or other local charges.

Unless specifically stated in your quotation or agreed upon in advance, these charges are not included in the product price or shipping cost.

We recommend checking your local import requirements before confirming your order.

Product Characteristics

Kyu Craft pop-up cards go through multiple stages of cutting, assembly, and finishing.

Because paper is a natural material and our products involve detailed production processes, there may occasionally be small variations in color, cutting position, assembly, or dimensions between production batches.

Minor variations that do not affect the overall design, appearance, or functionality of the product are considered normal characteristics of the manufacturing process.

Damaged or Defective Items

We take great care to inspect every wholesale order before it leaves our facility.

If any items arrive damaged or defective, please contact Kyu Craft within 7 days of receiving your shipment and provide:

  • Your order or invoice number
  • Quantity of affected items
  • Clear photos or videos showing the issue
  • A brief description of the problem

Our team will review the situation and work with you to find an appropriate solution. Depending on the circumstances, this may include replacement products, a refund, or credit toward a future order.

Returns

Because wholesale orders are produced and packed in quantity, our return policy is different from standard retail returns.

In particular, custom-designed, logo-printed, branded, or OEM products are generally non-returnable when they have been produced according to the design and specifications approved by the customer.

If there is a quality issue or your order differs from the confirmed specifications, please contact us and we’ll be happy to review the situation with you.

Order Changes & Cancellations

Need to make a change? Please contact us as soon as possible, and we’ll do our best to help.

Before production begins, changes or cancellations may be possible depending on the status of your order.

Once materials have been prepared or production has started, changes or cancellations may:

  • No longer be possible
  • Involve additional costs
  • Affect the production or delivery schedule

To help everything go smoothly, please carefully review your products, quantities, designs, branding details, packaging, and shipping information before approving production.

Artwork & Design Rights

For custom projects, customers are responsible for ensuring that any logos, artwork, images, text, or other materials provided to Kyu Craft are authorized for use in the requested project.

Kyu Craft will use these materials solely for the purpose of designing and producing your order.

Ownership and usage rights for exclusive OEM or custom-developed designs can be discussed and agreed upon separately for each project.
